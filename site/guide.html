📖 How to use the Results Portal

Shivneri Army Public School · one page for parents, teachers, the principal and the admin. ← Back to the portal

Parents See your child's report card

  1. Open the portal and tap 🎓 Parents login → Check a result.
  2. Choose the year, exam, class and section.
  3. Type the roll number, the child's name (as the school has it) and date of birth.
  4. Tap Show report card. You will also see progress across all exams. Tap 🖨 Print / Save PDF to keep a copy.
No result? Check the spelling of the name and the date of birth. If they are right, the result may not be published yet.

Teachers First login

  1. The admin adds your email first. Then open ✏️ Teacher login → First time? Set up your password.
  2. Type your email and a password (at least 8 characters, letters and numbers).
  3. Open the email from the school portal and click the link to confirm, then log in.
Forgot your password? Type your email on the login page and tap Forgot password?

Teachers Entering marks

  • One student at a time: on Results entry, choose the exam, class and section, pick the student from the list, type the marks and tap 💾 Save result.
  • Whole class from Excel: tap 📥 Excel marks, choose your subject, download the sheet, fill in the marks in Excel, save it as CSV, then upload it. If something is wrong (for example 95 out of 80), nothing is saved and the site tells you which row to fix.
  • You can change marks only for your subjects in your classes. Other subjects show 🔒.
  • Class teachers also add new students and fill in details, attendance, height/weight and remarks for their class.
  • 🖨 Print class prints (or saves as PDF) all report cards of the class for the chosen exam.
You see your own classes by default. Tap Show all classes at the top to view other classes.

Principal Dashboard and analysis

  • Dashboard: school average, students, A+ count, students below 50%, and marks still pending. Teacher spotlight shows the top teachers and those who may need support. Charts show grades, classes, subjects and exam-by-exam progress.
  • Find a teacher: on the Dashboard, search by name, subject, class or contract type. Tap a teacher to see her full profile: qualification, experience, date of joining, subjects, classes, results and contract details (type, renewal / re-tender date, termination date). Only the principal and admin can see or change contract details.
  • Contract reminders: renewals, re-tenders and terminations due in the next 60 days (or overdue) are listed at the top of the Dashboard.
  • Home page: the principal can change the school photo and her message (✏️ Edit and 📷 Change photo on the Home page).
  • Analysis: class overview, by subject, by exam, by student, teacher comparison and school toppers / support list.
  • Compared with school means a teacher's classes against all classes in the school in the same subject. Change is up ▲ or down ▼ from the exam just before (UT-I → Term I → UT-II → Term II).

Timetable Timetable, leave and substitutes

  • Week: Monday to Friday. Classes I and II have sections A–C; III, IV and V have A–B. 8 periods a day: 2 periods, short break, 2 periods, long break, 4 periods.
  • Fixed periods: Wednesday period 1 is Mass PT for the whole school; Friday periods 7–8 are CCA (Arts) or buffer. Nothing else is placed there. The CCA event for each Friday comes from the school calendar.
  • Coordinator: mark one teacher as coordinator on the Admin page; she is the first choice to fill in for an absent teacher.
  • Which subjects in which class: on the Admin page, after choosing a teacher's subjects and classes, tap the subjects for each class (e.g. English and EVS in I-A, Maths in II-C). Marks entry, the timetable and the database rules all follow this.
  • ✨ Set up (principal/admin): press ⚡ Set up everything once. It loads the school calendar, spaces the four exams and makes a clash-free timetable for all classes. Later you can press Remake timetable only (e.g. after staff changes) or Re-space exams only. Settings such as periods a week and timings are tucked under "Change the rules".
  • 📍 Today (principal): pick a day with ◀ ▶. You see who is on leave, the CCA or exam of the day, and the substitutes the site has already picked (the coordinator first). Change any, then 📢 Publish. Tap a period under "Who is free?" to see free teachers.
  • 🙋 My week (teachers): your timetable and any extra duty for the chosen day.
  • 📅 Classes: any class's week. The principal can tap a period, then another, to swap them; the site stops a swap if the teacher is busy elsewhere.
  • 🏖 Leave: teachers apply; the principal approves, rejects, or adds leave directly.
  • 📆 Calendar: what's coming up, exam date sheets, holidays and breaks, CCA Fridays and important days.

Admin Setting up and keeping data safe

  • Admin → Add or edit staff: name, email, role, qualification, experience, date of joining, photo, subjects, classes and class-teacher class.
  • Backups: tap ⬇ Download backup after every exam's marks are entered and keep the file safe (for example on Google Drive). The Admin page reminds you if it has been more than 30 days.
  • Removing demo data: 🧹 Delete sample (dummy) data removes all demo students and demo teachers.
  • Change your password with 🔑 Change password. Never share the admin login.

🔒 Privacy: how your child's data is kept

  • The portal stores what is on the report card: name, class, roll number, date of birth, parents' names, category, marks, attendance, height/weight, remarks and an optional photo. It is used only to publish results to the child's family and for the school's own academic review.
  • A report card opens only when the exact class, roll number, name and date of birth are given. Nobody can browse or list students.
  • Full records are visible only to school staff with a login. Teachers can change only their own subjects; every change records who made it.
  • Data is stored with Google Firebase. Parents can ask the school to correct details or remove a photo.
This note is a starting point. The school may wish to review it against its own policies and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act.
