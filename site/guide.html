📖 How to use the school website

Shivneri Army Public School · one page for parents, teachers, the principal and the admin. ← Back to the portal

Parents See your child's report card

  1. Open the portal and tap 🎓 Parents login → Check a result.
  2. Choose the year, exam, class and section.
  3. Type the roll number, the child's name (as the school has it) and date of birth.
  4. Tap Show report card. You will also see progress across all exams. Tap 🖨 Print / Save PDF to keep a copy.
No result? Check the spelling of the name and the date of birth. If they are right, the result may not be published yet.

Teachers First login

  1. The admin adds your email first. Then open ✏️ Teacher login → First time? Set up your password.
  2. Type your email and a password (at least 8 characters, letters and numbers).
  3. Open the email from the school portal and click the link to confirm, then log in.
Forgot your password? Type your email on the login page and tap Forgot password?

Teachers Entering marks

  • One student at a time: on Results entry, choose the exam, class and section, pick the student from the list, type the marks and tap 💾 Save result.
  • Whole class from Excel: tap 📥 Excel marks, choose your subject, download the sheet, fill in the marks in Excel, save it as CSV, then upload it. If something is wrong (for example 95 out of 80), nothing is saved and the site tells you which row to fix.
  • You can change marks only for your subjects in your classes. Other subjects show 🔒.
  • Class teachers also add new students and fill in details, attendance, height/weight and remarks for their class.
  • 🖨 Print class prints (or saves as PDF) all report cards of the class for the chosen exam.
You see your own classes by default. Tap Show all classes at the top to view other classes.

Principal Dashboard and analysis

  • Dashboard: school average, students, A+ count, students below 50%, and marks still pending. Teacher spotlight shows the top teachers and those who may need support. Charts show grades, classes, subjects and exam-by-exam progress.
  • Find a teacher: on the Dashboard, search by name, subject, class or contract type. Tap a teacher to see her full profile: qualification, experience, date of joining, subjects, classes, results and contract details (type, renewal / re-tender date, termination date). Only the principal and admin can see or change contract details.
  • Contract reminders: renewals, re-tenders and terminations due in the next 60 days (or overdue) are listed at the top of the Dashboard.
  • Home page: the principal can change the school photo and her message (✏️ Edit and 📷 Change photo on the Home page).
  • Analysis: class overview, by subject, by exam, by student, teacher comparison and school toppers / support list.
  • Compared with school means a teacher's classes against all classes in the school in the same subject. Change is up ▲ or down ▼ from the exam just before (UT-I → Term I → UT-II → Term II).

New Fees

  • Parents login now shows the child's fee status (each quarter: due date, amount, paid or overdue, receipt no.) and how to pay (bank, UPI, fee counter), above the report card.
  • 💳 Fees (Students page, coordinator and principal): pick a class and tap a quarter to record a payment with today's date, the payment mode and the next receipt no. Tap a paid one to undo a mistake.
  • Quarterly reminder: from 15 days before each quarter until 45 days after its due date, a fee reminder runs on the Home page strip and in Notices. The Fees tab shows how many have not paid, and 📋 Copy reminder list copies a ready message with the names (and phone numbers) to paste into WhatsApp or SMS.
  • ⚙️ Fee plan: due dates, fee per quarter for each class, late fee and payment details. The coordinator's changes need the principal's approval.

New Admissions, notices and achievements

  • 🎒 Admissions (Students → Admissions): only the coordinator (or principal) admits a child. The section with the fewest children and the next roll and admission no. are suggested. The coordinator's admissions wait for the principal (✅ Approve admission in the approval box). Class teachers cannot add new children; when they type a roll no. in Results entry, the child's details fill in from the register.
  • 📢 Notices (public): admissions, exams, PTMs, events like Annual Day, guest visits and holidays. Upcoming exams from the school calendar show on top. For events, add staff duties (who does what); only staff see them, and each teacher sees "Your event duties".
  • 🏆 Achievements (public): students, teams and staff, with an optional photo. Use a child's full name or photo only with the parent's consent.
  • The newest notices and achievements flash on the Home page in a moving strip and two cards.
  • Notices, events with duties and achievements made by the coordinator go live only after the principal approves them.

Students Student profiles, attendance and notes

  • Coordinator login: the coordinator has her own door on the Home page and does the office work: students, attendance, consent, notes and records directly; the timetable, calendar, substitutes, exam duty, leave and the disclosure page as drafts. The principal sees them under 📥 Waiting for your approval (Dashboard, Timetable, Disclosure) and presses Approve and publish or Send back. On the Office page she can also recommend staff changes (add, edit or remove teachers, mark the coordinator), teachers' contracts, the principal's message and school photo, a data import, and deleting sample or old data; all of these wait for the principal's approval. Teachers' analysis and the dashboard are only for the principal and admin. Only the admin can add, change or remove the principal, and the coordinator never sees the principal's own contract.
  • 🔒 Consent and privacy: record the parent's signed consent on each student's profile (🖨 print the form there). The photo shows on the parents' report card only if the parent agreed. A warning lists students with no consent yet. For a child who has left, the office can ⬇ download and then 🗑 erase all online data. The public Privacy page is linked in the footer.
  • 📋 Mandatory Public Disclosure: the CBSE Appendix IX page (general information, documents as https links, staff with qualifications, infrastructure, fees, committees including POCSO and POSH) and a TC check, open to everyone. Marking a student as left with a TC no. adds it to the TC check, which needs both the TC no. and the name, so no list of children can be browsed.
  • 🔎 Find a student: search by name, roll no., admission no. or a parent's name. The profile shows parents, category, attendance, all exam results, health and discipline notes, and history.
  • Parents' details: only the names and the category are kept. For serving parents just Officer / JCO / OR; the exact rank and unit are not recorded, for security. A contact phone can be saved for staff only.
  • ➕ Add a new student: opens Results entry for that class with the next roll no.
  • 🔀 Transfer section (principal / coordinator): moves the student and all this year's results to the new section and roll no. Parents then use the new section and roll no.
  • 🚪 Mark as left the school (principal / coordinator): date, reason and TC no. Results stay saved; a "Left" tag shows in class lists, and the student drops out of attendance. Re-admit undoes it.
  • Relief for absent teachers: when the coordinator marks a teacher absent in Staff attendance (including herself), or recommends a leave, the 🔁 Relief / Plan relief button opens that day's substitute plan with the absent teacher's periods already filled by free teachers. Check it and publish.
  • Teacher details: the coordinator can open any teacher's 📄 Details (Staff attendance or Office page): attendance for the year, personal details (mobile, date of birth, blood group, address, emergency contact) and contract. Her changes go to the principal for approval; she never sees the principal's own.
  • ✅ Class attendance: the class teacher (or another teacher of that class when she is away) picks the date and taps the absent children, then saves. Monthly percentages show below; below 75% is flagged on the profile.
  • 👩‍🏫 Staff attendance (principal / coordinator): tap to switch Present → Absent → Late. Approved leave fills in by itself.
  • 🩺 Health and ⚖️ discipline: seen only by the teachers of that class, the coordinator and the principal; written by the class teacher, coordinator or principal. Never shown to parents.

Timetable Timetable, leave and substitutes

  • Week: Monday to Friday. Classes I and II have sections A–C; III, IV and V have A–B. 8 periods a day: 2 periods, short break, 2 periods, long break, 4 periods.
  • Fixed periods: Wednesday period 1 is Mass PT for the whole school; Friday periods 7–8 are CCA (Arts) or buffer. Nothing else is placed there. The CCA event for each Friday comes from the school calendar.
  • Coordinator: mark one teacher as coordinator on the Admin page; she is the first choice to fill in for an absent teacher.
  • Which subjects in which class: on the Admin page, after choosing a teacher's subjects and classes, tap the subjects for each class (e.g. English and EVS in I-A, Maths in II-C). Marks entry, the timetable and the database rules all follow this.
  • ✨ Set up (principal/admin): press ⚡ Set up everything once. It loads the school calendar, spaces the four exams and makes a clash-free timetable for all classes. Later you can press Remake timetable only (e.g. after staff changes) or Re-space exams only. Settings such as periods a week and timings are tucked under "Change the rules".
  • 📍 Today (principal): pick a day with ◀ ▶. You see who is on leave, the CCA or exam of the day, and the substitutes the site has already picked (the coordinator first). Change any, then 📢 Publish. Tap a period under "Who is free?" to see free teachers.
  • 🙋 My week (teachers): your timetable and any extra duty for the chosen day.
  • 📅 Classes: any class's week. The principal can tap a period, then another, to swap them; the site stops a swap if the teacher is busy elsewhere.
  • 🏖 Leave: teachers apply; the principal or coordinator approves, rejects, or adds leave directly.
  • No class without a teacher: each class has 37 teaching periods a week but its subjects need 30, so the 7 extra periods become Library / Activity with the class teacher or the teacher with the lightest week. The coordinator is kept free for substitutions.
  • 🗂️ Coordinator relief (bottom of the Leave tab): when the coordinator has approved leave, the site suggests a teacher to act for her (lightest load, most experience, not on leave then). The principal presses Make acting coordinator. That teacher signs in with the Coordinator login and has the coordinator's powers only for those dates; it switches off by itself.
  • 📝 Exams: the date sheet for each exam (all classes) and the invigilation duty. The principal presses ⚡ Assign invigilators: one teacher per class for each paper, never in her own subject or own class, teachers on leave left out, duties shared evenly, the coordinator kept as reserve. Change any room, then 📢 Publish; each teacher sees her own duties. 🖨 Print / Save PDF gives the date sheet and the duty chart; All four exams on one page gives the year's schedule.
  • 📆 Calendar: what's coming up, exam date sheets, holidays and breaks, CCA Fridays and important days.

Admin Setting up and keeping data safe

  • Admin → Add or edit staff: name, email, role, qualification, experience, date of joining, photo, subjects, classes and class-teacher class.
  • Backups: tap ⬇ Download backup after every exam's marks are entered and keep the file safe (for example on Google Drive). The Admin page reminds you if it has been more than 30 days.
  • Removing demo data: 🧹 Delete sample (dummy) data removes all demo students and demo teachers.
  • Change your password with 🔑 Change password. Never share the admin login.

🔒 Privacy: how your child's data is kept

  • The portal stores what is on the report card: name, class, roll number, date of birth, parents' names, category, marks, attendance, height/weight, remarks and an optional photo. It is used only to publish results to the child's family and for the school's own academic review.
  • A report card opens only when the exact class, roll number, name and date of birth are given. Nobody can browse or list students.
  • Full records are visible only to school staff with a login. Teachers can change only their own subjects; every change records who made it.
  • Data is stored with Google Firebase. Parents can ask the school to correct details or remove a photo.
This note is a starting point. The school may wish to review it against its own policies and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act.
